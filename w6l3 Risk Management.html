Introduction: The Case

  • Digital age = huge opportunities + significant vulnerabilities, even for businesses with deep roots.
  • Newhouse Cheese Company, founded 1811 ➜ a venerable institution.
  • After centuries of traditional operations: extensive digitalization in the early 2000s under 5th-generation CEO Chadwick Newhouse: heavy investment in precision control monitors & devices.
  • Result: efficiency & cost savings ✅ but exposed to sophisticated cyber threats ❌.
  • Lecture: aftermath of a crippling ransomware attack ➜ company paid a substantial sum to regain control.
  • Real-world lesson: industrial control systems, once isolated, are now prime targets ➜ consequences from financial loss to public health crises.
  • Needs a comprehensive approach: technology advancement ⚖️ robust security, often re-evaluating the very processes that drive modern industry.

Hybrid (Manual + Digital) Approach: Pros & Cons

Question to the audience: Should Chad implement the consultant's recommendations? Consensus: an "in-between solution": don't abandon digitalization, but strategically move critical assets offline or restrict access. Cast your vote:

(Votes are just for you, nothing is saved.)

👍 Pros

  • Isolation & prevention: critical systems isolated ➜ less damage (theory of least privileges: limits attacker access to the entire system)
  • Production continuity: manual backup/parallel system keeps production going
  • Sensitive data security: offline data is safer from digital access
  • Reduced unauthorized access: fewer digital pathways/access points

👎 Cons

  • Sunk cost: big investment in automation wasted if reverted
  • Reputation & investor trust may suffer
  • Increased operational cost: hiring, training, skills
  • Initial operational hindrance: setting up parallel manual systems
  • Human error returns: the main reason for digitalizing
💡
The big challenge: balancing Industry 4.0 & IoT benefits with severe risks (financial loss and potential loss of life) when industrial controls are hit.

    Conclusion

    • Newhouse Cheese: ransomware crisis ➜ strategic re-evaluation of its digital footprint = a universal challenge.
    • Efficiency & cost savings from digitalization/IoT vs exposing critical operational technology to sophisticated threats.
    • Broader struggle: harness Industry 4.0 without sacrificing security & safety.
